# Bank 1 — Most-Repeated General Paper MCQs (with answers)

> **Paper:** NPSC CTSE — General English & General Knowledge (Combined), 100 marks.
> **What this is:** 80 questions on the facts NPSC keeps coming back to across its GK papers
> (CTSE, CCE and other recruitment papers): Nagaland statehood and history, Article 371A,
> tribes and festivals, the Constitution's key Articles, the freedom-struggle timeline,
> and the standard English items (synonyms, idioms, one-word substitution, error spotting).
> The **answer is printed straight under each question.** Cover it with a finger to self-test.
>
> **Stars:** ★★★ = shows up in almost every paper · ★★ = frequently · ★ = regularly.
>
> **How "most repeated" was decided:** by the question *themes* that recur in NPSC GK papers.
> The exact wording changes every year. The facts don't. The NPSC site could not be reached
> from this environment, so no stem here is a word-for-word copy of a real paper.

---

## A. Nagaland — statehood & political history

**1.** ★★★ Nagaland became a full-fledged state of India on:  
(a) 26 January 1950 (b) 1 December 1963 (c) 21 January 1972 (d) 1 December 1960
> **Ans: (b) 1 December 1963.** It was the 16th state and was inaugurated by President Dr. S. Radhakrishnan.

**2.** ★★★ Nagaland was the ___ state of the Indian Union.  
(a) 14th (b) 15th (c) 16th (d) 17th
> **Ans: (c) 16th.**

**3.** ★★★ The Act of Parliament that created the State of Nagaland is:  
(a) State of Nagaland Act, 1962 (b) North-Eastern Areas (Reorganisation) Act, 1971 (c) Assam Reorganisation Act, 1963 (d) Nagaland Statehood Act, 1963
> **Ans: (a) State of Nagaland Act, 1962.** It came into force on 1 Dec 1963.

**4.** ★★★ Special constitutional provisions for Nagaland are contained in:  
(a) Article 370 (b) Article 371 (c) Article 371A (d) Article 371G
> **Ans: (c) Article 371A**, inserted by the **13th Amendment Act, 1962**.

**5.** ★★★ Which of these is NOT protected under Article 371A (without a resolution of the Nagaland Legislative Assembly)?  
(a) Religious or social practices of the Nagas (b) Naga customary law and procedure (c) Ownership and transfer of land and its resources (d) Collection of income tax
> **Ans: (d).** The four protected matters are religious/social practices, customary law and procedure, civil/criminal justice under customary law, and ownership/transfer of land and its resources.

**6.** ★★★ The first Chief Minister of Nagaland was:  
(a) T. N. Angami (b) P. Shilu Ao (c) Hokishe Sema (d) S. C. Jamir
> **Ans: (b) P. Shilu Ao** (1963–66).

**7.** ★★ The first Governor of Nagaland was:  
(a) Vishnu Sahay (b) B. K. Nehru (c) L. P. Singh (d) M. M. Thomas
> **Ans: (a) Vishnu Sahay.**

**8.** ★★★ The Naga Club, the first Naga political organisation, was formed in:  
(a) 1918 (b) 1929 (c) 1946 (d) 1951
> **Ans: (a) 1918**, at Kohima.

**9.** ★★★ In 1929 the Naga Club submitted a memorandum to:  
(a) Cripps Mission (b) Simon Commission (c) Cabinet Mission (d) Montagu–Chelmsford
> **Ans: (b) Simon Commission.**

**10.** ★★★ The Naga National Council (NNC) was formed in:  
(a) 1918 (b) 1946 (c) 1947 (d) 1957
> **Ans: (b) 1946** (at Wokha, February 1946).

**11.** ★★ The Nine-Point Agreement of June 1947 between the NNC and the Governor of Assam is known as the:  
(a) Shillong Accord (b) Hydari Agreement (c) 16-Point Agreement (d) Simla Agreement
> **Ans: (b) Hydari Agreement**, after Sir Akbar Hydari, Governor of Assam.

**12.** ★★★ The 16-Point Agreement that led to statehood was signed in:  
(a) 1957 (b) 1960 (c) 1962 (d) 1963
> **Ans: (b) 1960** (July 1960), between the Naga People's Convention (NPC) and the Government of India.

**13.** ★★ The Naga Hills–Tuensang Area (NHTA) was created in:  
(a) 1947 (b) 1957 (c) 1960 (d) 1963
> **Ans: (b) 1957.** It was formed after the first Naga People's Convention.

**14.** ★★ The Shillong Accord was signed in:  
(a) 1963 (b) 1972 (c) 1975 (d) 1997
> **Ans: (c) 1975** (11 November 1975).

**15.** ★★ The Framework Agreement between the Government of India and NSCN (I-M) was signed on:  
(a) 1 Aug 1997 (b) 3 Aug 2015 (c) 15 Aug 2015 (d) 1 Dec 2013
> **Ans: (b) 3 August 2015.** (The ceasefire with NSCN-IM dates from 1997.)

**16.** ★★★ The Battle of Kohima (1944) was fought between:  
(a) British-Indian forces and the Japanese (b) Nagas and the British (c) India and China (d) Burma and the British
> **Ans: (a).** It was fought in WWII (April–June 1944) and is famous for the fighting on the District Commissioner's **tennis court**.

**17.** ★★ "When you go home, tell them of us and say, for your tomorrow, we gave our today" is inscribed at:  
(a) India Gate (b) Kohima War Cemetery (c) Imphal War Cemetery (d) Cellular Jail
> **Ans: (b) Kohima War Cemetery** (the Kohima Epitaph, by John Maxwell Edmonds).

**18.** ★★★ Rani Gaidinliu, the Naga freedom fighter, was given the title "Rani" by:  
(a) Mahatma Gandhi (b) Jawaharlal Nehru (c) Subhas Chandra Bose (d) Sardar Patel
> **Ans: (b) Jawaharlal Nehru.** She was a Zeliangrong leader who succeeded Haipou Jadonang.

## B. Nagaland — profile, geography & culture

**19.** ★★★ The highest peak in Nagaland is:  
(a) Japfü (b) Saramati (c) Dzükou (d) Pauna
> **Ans: (b) Mount Saramati** (~3,841 m), in Kiphire district on the Myanmar border.

**20.** ★★★ The longest river of Nagaland is:  
(a) Dhansiri (b) Dikhu (c) Doyang (d) Tizu
> **Ans: (c) Doyang.**

**21.** ★★★ The Hornbill Festival is held every year from:  
(a) 1–7 November (b) 1–10 December (c) 25 Dec–1 Jan (d) 1–10 January
> **Ans: (b) 1–10 December**, at the Naga Heritage Village, **Kisama**. It began in **2000**.

**22.** ★★★ The state animal of Nagaland is the:  
(a) Hoolock gibbon (b) Mithun (c) Clouded leopard (d) Red panda
> **Ans: (b) Mithun** (*Bos frontalis*).

**23.** ★★★ The state bird of Nagaland is:  
(a) Great Indian Hornbill (b) Blyth's Tragopan (c) Amur Falcon (d) Hill Myna
> **Ans: (b) Blyth's Tragopan.** (The state flower is Rhododendron and the state tree is Alder.)

**24.** ★★★ The official language of Nagaland is:  
(a) Nagamese (b) Hindi (c) English (d) Assamese
> **Ans: (c) English.**

**25.** ★★★ Seats in the Nagaland Legislative Assembly:  
(a) 40 (b) 50 (c) 60 (d) 70
> **Ans: (c) 60.** Nagaland has **1** Lok Sabha seat and **1** Rajya Sabha seat.

**26.** ★★★ Nagaland shares an international border with:  
(a) Bangladesh (b) Bhutan (c) China (d) Myanmar
> **Ans: (d) Myanmar.** Its state neighbours are Assam, Arunachal Pradesh and Manipur.

**27.** ★★★ Sekrenyi is the festival of the:  
(a) Ao (b) Angami (c) Sumi (d) Lotha
> **Ans: (b) Angami** (February).

**28.** ★★★ Moatsü is the festival of the:  
(a) Ao (b) Konyak (c) Chang (d) Phom
> **Ans: (a) Ao** (first week of May, 1–3 May).

**29.** ★★ Tuluni is celebrated by the:  
(a) Lotha (b) Sumi (c) Rengma (d) Zeliang
> **Ans: (b) Sumi**, in July. The Sumi's other festival is Ahuna (November).

**30.** ★★ Aoleang is the festival of the:  
(a) Konyak (b) Khiamniungan (c) Yimkhiung (d) Sangtam
> **Ans: (a) Konyak** (first week of April).

**31.** ★★ Tokhü Emong is the festival of the:  
(a) Lotha (b) Ao (c) Pochury (d) Chakhesang
> **Ans: (a) Lotha** (7 November).

**32.** ★★★ India's first "Green Village" in Nagaland is:  
(a) Longwa (b) Khonoma (c) Touphema (d) Chizami
> **Ans: (b) Khonoma**, in Kohima district.

**33.** ★★ Pangti village in Wokha is famous as the "Falcon Capital of the World" because of:  
(a) Peregrine falcons (b) Amur falcons (c) Hornbills (d) Siberian cranes
> **Ans: (b) Amur falcons.** They roost around the Doyang reservoir in Oct–Nov.

**34.** ★★ The only railway station and airport in Nagaland for decades has been at:  
(a) Kohima (b) Mokokchung (c) Dimapur (d) Wokha
> **Ans: (c) Dimapur.** (A second station, Shokhuvi, opened on the Dhansiri–Zubza line in 2021.)

**35.** ★★ The only National Park of Nagaland is:  
(a) Fakim (b) Intanki (c) Puliebadze (d) Rangapahar
> **Ans: (b) Intanki National Park** (Peren district).

**36.** ★★ Nagaland University has its headquarters at:  
(a) Kohima (b) Dimapur (c) Lumami (d) Medziphema
> **Ans: (c) Lumami** (Zunheboto district). It was set up in 1994.

**37.** ★★ The Kachari ruins, remains of the Dimasa Kachari kingdom, are located at:  
(a) Kohima (b) Dimapur (c) Mon (d) Peren
> **Ans: (b) Dimapur.**

**38.** ★★ Longwa village, where the Angh's house straddles the India–Myanmar border, is in:  
(a) Tuensang (b) Mon (c) Kiphire (d) Phek
> **Ans: (b) Mon district** (Konyak territory).

**39.** ★★ The Nagaland Communitisation of Public Institutions and Services Act was passed in:  
(a) 1978 (b) 1995 (c) 2002 (d) 2010
> **Ans: (c) 2002.**

**40.** ★ The first two women elected to the Nagaland Legislative Assembly (2023) were:  
(a) Rano Shaiza & S. Phangnon Konyak (b) Hekani Jakhalu & Salhoutuonuo Kruse (c) Rano Shaiza & Hekani Jakhalu (d) Salhoutuonuo Kruse & Phangnon Konyak
> **Ans: (b).** Salhoutuonuo Kruse became the first woman minister. (Rano M. Shaiza was Nagaland's first woman Lok Sabha MP in 1977, and S. Phangnon Konyak its first woman Rajya Sabha MP in 2022.)

## C. Indian Polity

**41.** ★★★ Right to Constitutional Remedies, called the "heart and soul" of the Constitution, is:  
(a) Art. 14 (b) Art. 19 (c) Art. 21 (d) Art. 32
> **Ans: (d) Article 32.**

**42.** ★★★ The Constitution of India came into force on:  
(a) 26 Nov 1949 (b) 26 Jan 1950 (c) 15 Aug 1947 (d) 9 Dec 1946
> **Ans: (b) 26 January 1950.** It was *adopted* on 26 Nov 1949, now observed as Constitution Day.

**43.** ★★★ The Chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Constitution was:  
(a) Rajendra Prasad (b) B. R. Ambedkar (c) Jawaharlal Nehru (d) B. N. Rau
> **Ans: (b) Dr. B. R. Ambedkar.** Rajendra Prasad was President of the Constituent Assembly.

**44.** ★★★ Fundamental Duties were added by the:  
(a) 42nd Amendment, 1976 (b) 44th Amendment, 1978 (c) 52nd Amendment, 1985 (d) 86th Amendment, 2002
> **Ans: (a) 42nd Amendment**, as Article 51A, on the Swaran Singh Committee's recommendation.

**45.** ★★★ The idea of Directive Principles of State Policy was borrowed from the constitution of:  
(a) USA (b) UK (c) Ireland (d) Canada
> **Ans: (c) Ireland.**

**46.** ★★★ President's Rule in a state is imposed under:  
(a) Art. 352 (b) Art. 356 (c) Art. 360 (d) Art. 368
> **Ans: (b) Article 356.** (352 is National Emergency, 360 Financial Emergency, 368 amendment procedure.)

**47.** ★★★ The minimum age to be elected President of India is:  
(a) 25 (b) 30 (c) 35 (d) 40
> **Ans: (c) 35 years.** (Lok Sabha 25, Rajya Sabha 30.)

**48.** ★★★ The Anti-Defection Law (Tenth Schedule) was added by the:  
(a) 42nd (b) 44th (c) 52nd (d) 61st Amendment
> **Ans: (c) 52nd Amendment, 1985.**

**49.** ★★★ The voting age was lowered from 21 to 18 by the:  
(a) 42nd (b) 52nd (c) 61st (d) 73rd Amendment
> **Ans: (c) 61st Amendment** (1988, effective 1989).

**50.** ★★★ Right to Education (Art. 21A) was inserted by the:  
(a) 73rd (b) 86th (c) 91st (d) 101st Amendment
> **Ans: (b) 86th Amendment, 2002.**

**51.** ★★ The Sixth Schedule (autonomous district councils) applies to tribal areas of:  
(a) Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram (b) Nagaland, Manipur, Assam, Tripura (c) Arunachal, Nagaland, Mizoram, Sikkim (d) All 8 NE states
> **Ans: (a).** It is a classic trap: **Nagaland is NOT under the Sixth Schedule.** It has Article 371A instead.

## D. History & Freedom Struggle

**52.** ★★★ The Indian National Congress was founded in 1885 by:  
(a) Dadabhai Naoroji (b) A. O. Hume (c) W. C. Bonnerjee (d) Surendranath Banerjee
> **Ans: (b) A. O. Hume.** The first session was at Bombay, presided over by W. C. Bonnerjee.

**53.** ★★★ The Jallianwala Bagh massacre took place on:  
(a) 13 April 1919 (b) 13 April 1920 (c) 6 April 1930 (d) 8 August 1942
> **Ans: (a) 13 April 1919**, at Amritsar, under General Dyer.

**54.** ★★★ The Dandi March (Salt Satyagraha) began in:  
(a) 1920 (b) 1930 (c) 1942 (d) 1919
> **Ans: (b) 1930** (12 March–6 April), from Sabarmati Ashram.

**55.** ★★★ "Do or Die" was the slogan of the:  
(a) Non-Cooperation Movement (b) Civil Disobedience Movement (c) Quit India Movement (d) Swadeshi Movement
> **Ans: (c) Quit India Movement**, launched on 8 August 1942.

**56.** ★★ The First Battle of Panipat (1526) was fought between:  
(a) Babur and Ibrahim Lodi (b) Akbar and Hemu (c) Abdali and the Marathas (d) Babur and Rana Sanga
> **Ans: (a).** The Second (1556) was Akbar vs Hemu; the Third (1761) was Ahmad Shah Abdali vs the Marathas.

## E. Economy, Geography & Science

**57.** ★★★ The Reserve Bank of India was established in:  
(a) 1935 (b) 1947 (c) 1949 (d) 1969
> **Ans: (a) 1 April 1935.** It was nationalised in 1949.

**58.** ★★★ GST was rolled out in India on:  
(a) 1 April 2017 (b) 1 July 2017 (c) 8 Nov 2016 (d) 1 Jan 2018
> **Ans: (b) 1 July 2017**, through the 101st Amendment.

**59.** ★★★ NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission on:  
(a) 1 Jan 2015 (b) 1 Apr 2015 (c) 15 Aug 2014 (d) 26 Jan 2015
> **Ans: (a) 1 January 2015.** The Prime Minister is its chairperson.

**60.** ★★★ The Tropic of Cancer does NOT pass through:  
(a) Mizoram (b) Tripura (c) Manipur (d) Jharkhand
> **Ans: (c) Manipur.** It passes through 8 states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram.

**61.** ★★★ The largest river island in the world, Majuli, is on the river:  
(a) Ganga (b) Brahmaputra (c) Barak (d) Godavari
> **Ans: (b) Brahmaputra** (Assam).

**62.** ★★ Keibul Lamjao, the only floating national park in the world, is in:  
(a) Nagaland (b) Assam (c) Manipur (d) Mizoram
> **Ans: (c) Manipur**, on Loktak Lake (its floating mats are called *phumdis*). It is home to the Sangai deer.

**63.** ★★★ Deficiency of Vitamin C causes:  
(a) Rickets (b) Scurvy (c) Beriberi (d) Night blindness
> **Ans: (b) Scurvy.** (D causes rickets, B1 beriberi, A night blindness.)

**64.** ★★★ The universal blood donor group is:  
(a) A (b) B (c) AB (d) O
> **Ans: (d) O (negative).** AB+ is the universal recipient.

**65.** ★★ The chemical formula of baking soda is:  
(a) Na₂CO₃ (b) NaHCO₃ (c) NaCl (d) CaOCl₂
> **Ans: (b) NaHCO₃** (sodium bicarbonate). Na₂CO₃ is washing soda and CaOCl₂ is bleaching powder.

**66.** ★★ The "powerhouse of the cell" is the:  
(a) Nucleus (b) Ribosome (c) Mitochondria (d) Golgi body
> **Ans: (c) Mitochondria.**

## F. General English (repeated item types)

**67.** ★★★ Synonym of **CANDID**:  
(a) Secretive (b) Frank (c) Cunning (d) Shy
> **Ans: (b) Frank.**

**68.** ★★★ Synonym of **EPHEMERAL**:  
(a) Eternal (b) Short-lived (c) Ancient (d) Heavenly
> **Ans: (b) Short-lived.**

**69.** ★★★ Antonym of **ABUNDANCE**:  
(a) Plenty (b) Scarcity (c) Wealth (d) Excess
> **Ans: (b) Scarcity.**

**70.** ★★★ Idiom — "**to burn the midnight oil**":  
(a) waste resources (b) work or study late into the night (c) start a fire (d) celebrate
> **Ans: (b).**

**71.** ★★★ Idiom — "**once in a blue moon**":  
(a) very often (b) very rarely (c) at night (d) monthly
> **Ans: (b) very rarely.**

**72.** ★★★ Idiom — "**to let the cat out of the bag**":  
(a) to free an animal (b) to reveal a secret (c) to cause trouble (d) to escape
> **Ans: (b) to reveal a secret.**

**73.** ★★★ One word: *A person who does not believe in the existence of God*:  
(a) Theist (b) Atheist (c) Agnostic (d) Heretic
> **Ans: (b) Atheist.** (An agnostic holds that God's existence cannot be known.)

**74.** ★★★ One word: *Life history of a person written by himself/herself*:  
(a) Biography (b) Autobiography (c) Memoir (d) Diary
> **Ans: (b) Autobiography.**

**75.** ★★★ One word: *A speech delivered without preparation*:  
(a) Extempore (b) Soliloquy (c) Monologue (d) Rhetoric
> **Ans: (a) Extempore.**

**76.** ★★★ Spot the error: "One of my **friend** / is working / in Kohima."  
(a) One of my friend (b) is working (c) in Kohima (d) No error
> **Ans: (a)** → "One of my **friends**." *One of* always takes a plural noun.

**77.** ★★★ Spot the error: "He is **senior than** me / in the office."  
(a) He is senior than me (b) in the office (c) No error
> **Ans: (a)** → "senior **to** me." *Senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior* take **to**.

**78.** ★★★ Choose the correct verb: "The news ___ very good today."  
(a) are (b) is (c) were (d) have been
> **Ans: (b) is.** *News* is singular.

**79.** ★★★ Fill in the article: "He is ___ honest man."  
(a) a (b) an (c) the (d) no article
> **Ans: (b) an.** *Honest* begins with a vowel **sound** (the h is silent).

**80.** ★★★ Choose the correctly spelt word:  
(a) Accomodation (b) Accommodation (c) Acommodation (d) Accommadation
> **Ans: (b) Accommodation** (double c, double m).
