> **What this is:** 75 questions on the topics most likely to be asked this year:
> - **Part A:** current affairs from Sep 2025 to mid-2026 (NPSC opens GK with a block on the last 12 months)
> - **Part B:** Nagaland topics that are "live" right now and haven't been asked much yet
> - **Part C:** mental ability, the type NPSC includes
> - **Part D:** General English, the likely item types
>
> The answer is printed directly under each question.
>
> **⚠️ Read this first.**

## PART A — Current affairs (Sep 2025 → mid-2026)

### A1. Offices & appointments

**1.** ⚠️ Who was elected the 15th Vice-President of India in September 2025, after Jagdeep Dhankhar resigned?  
(a) Om Birla (b) C. P. Radhakrishnan (c) B. Sudershan Reddy (d) Harivansh
> **Ans: (b) C. P. Radhakrishnan.** He defeated B. Sudershan Reddy.

**2.** ⚠️ Who became the 53rd Chief Justice of India in November 2025?  
(a) B. R. Gavai (b) Sanjiv Khanna (c) Surya Kant (d) Vikram Nath
> **Ans: (c) Justice Surya Kant**, who succeeded Justice B. R. Gavai.

**3.** The Governor of the Reserve Bank of India (since December 2024) is:  
(a) Shaktikanta Das (b) Sanjay Malhotra (c) Urjit Patel (d) Michael Patra
> **Ans: (b) Sanjay Malhotra** (26th Governor).

**4.** The Chief Election Commissioner of India (since February 2025) is:  
(a) Rajiv Kumar (b) Gyanesh Kumar (c) Sukhbir Singh Sandhu (d) Sushil Chandra
> **Ans: (b) Gyanesh Kumar.**

**5.** The Chairman of the 16th Finance Commission (award period 2026–31) is:  
(a) N. K. Singh (b) Arvind Panagariya (c) Y. V. Reddy (d) Bibek Debroy
> **Ans: (b) Arvind Panagariya.**

**6.** ⚠️ **Verify tonight:** the present Governor and Chief Minister of Nagaland. Governor La. Ganesan died in August 2025, and the office has changed since. Neiphiu Rio is CM (5th term, sworn in March 2023). Also check which party Rio now leads: NDPP merged into NPF in late 2025.  
> **Ans: check a newspaper.** This is a near-certain question type, so don't go in with a stale name.

### A2. Economy & governance

**7.** Under "GST 2.0", in effect from 22 September 2025, the main GST slabs were rationalised to:  
(a) 5% and 12% (b) 5% and 18% (c) 12% and 18% (d) 12% and 28%
> **Ans: (b) 5% and 18%**, plus a special 40% rate on sin/luxury goods.

**8.** ⚠️ India's four Labour Codes (Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, Occupational Safety) came into effect in:  
(a) April 2020 (b) November 2025 (c) January 2024 (d) July 2017
> **Ans: (b) 21 November 2025.** Together they replaced 29 central labour laws.

**9.** ⚠️ The next national Census (reference date 1 March 2027) is notable because it will:  
(a) be the first ever Census of India (b) be the first digital Census and include caste enumeration (c) exclude the North-East (d) be done only online by citizens
> **Ans: (b).** It is India's first digital Census and the first to count caste since independence. House-listing is in 2026, and snow-bound areas take the reference date 1 Oct 2026.

**10.** ⚠️ The Union Budget 2026-27 was presented on 1 February 2026 by:  
(a) Piyush Goyal (b) Nirmala Sitharaman (c) Arun Jaitley (d) Pankaj Chaudhary
> **Ans: (b) Nirmala Sitharaman**, her 9th consecutive budget.

### A3. Security, science & space

**11.** "Operation Sindoor" (May 2025) was India's military response to:  
(a) the Galwan clash (b) the Pahalgam terror attack (c) the Pulwama attack (d) the Uri attack
> **Ans: (b)** the Pahalgam attack of 22 April 2025. India also placed the **Indus Waters Treaty in abeyance**.

**12.** Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla made history in 2025 as the:  
(a) first Indian to walk on the Moon (b) first Indian to visit the International Space Station (c) first Indian woman in space (d) commander of Gaganyaan-1
> **Ans: (b).** He flew on the **Axiom-4** mission and is the 2nd Indian in space after Rakesh Sharma (1984).

**13.** NISAR, launched on 30 July 2025, is a joint earth-observation satellite of:  
(a) ISRO & ESA (b) ISRO & NASA (c) ISRO & JAXA (d) ISRO & Roscosmos
> **Ans: (b) ISRO & NASA.** It was launched on GSLV-F16 from Sriharikota and carries a dual-band (L & S) radar.

**14.** With the SpaDeX mission (January 2025), India became the ___ country to achieve space docking.  
(a) 2nd (b) 3rd (c) 4th (d) 5th
> **Ans: (c) 4th**, after the USA, Russia and China.

**15.** The Chenab Bridge, inaugurated in June 2025, is the world's:  
(a) longest sea bridge (b) highest railway arch bridge (c) longest suspension bridge (d) first vertical-lift bridge
> **Ans: (b) highest railway arch bridge**, on the Udhampur–Srinagar–Baramulla line in J&K. (The **new Pamban Bridge**, April 2025, is India's first vertical-lift sea bridge.)

**16.** In September 2025 Aizawl was linked to India's railway network by the new line:  
(a) Silchar–Jiribam (b) Bairabi–Sairang (c) Dimapur–Zubza (d) Agartala–Akhaura
> **Ans: (b) Bairabi–Sairang**, making Mizoram the latest NE state capital on the rail map. (Nagaland's Dhansiri–Zubza line towards Kohima is still being built. ⚠️)

**17.** ⚠️ The "AI Impact Summit" of February 2026 was hosted by:  
(a) France (b) UK (c) India (d) South Korea
> **Ans: (c) India (New Delhi).** It was the first global AI summit held in the Global South.

**22.** ⚠️ The chief guests at India's 77th Republic Day (26 January 2026) were leaders of:

## PART B — Nagaland: likely "fresh" questions

**33.** The Register of Indigenous Inhabitants of Nagaland (RIIN) uses which cut-off date for indigenous status?  
(a) 26 January 1950 (b) 1 December 1963 (c) 15 August 1947 (d) 1 January 1979
> **Ans: (b) 1 December 1963**, the date of statehood.

**34.** The Inner Line Permit (ILP) system in Nagaland comes from the:  
(a) Government of India Act, 1935 (b) Bengal Eastern Frontier Regulation, 1873 (c) Assam Frontier Tract Regulation, 1880 (d) AFSPA, 1958
> **Ans: (b) BEFR, 1873.** It was extended to **Dimapur** in December 2019.

**35.** The Nagaland Liquor Total Prohibition (NLTP) Act was enacted in:  
(a) 1963 (b) 1978 (c) 1989 (d) 2002
> **Ans: (c) 1989.**

**36.** The Nagaland Village and Area Councils Act, which gives village councils statutory status, was passed in:  
(a) 1963 (b) 1978 (c) 1989 (d) 2002
> **Ans: (b) 1978.**

**37.** ⚠️ The Eastern Nagaland Peoples' Organisation (ENPO) has been demanding a separate:  
(a) "Frontier Nagaland Territory" (b) "Nagalim" (c) Union Territory of Tuensang (d) Sixth Schedule council
> **Ans: (a) Frontier Nagaland Territory.** A **Frontier Nagaland Territorial Authority (FNTA)** was proposed for the six eastern districts. ⚠️ Check the latest status.

**38.** ⚠️ Which of these was among the new districts created in Nagaland in December 2021?  
(a) Kiphire (b) Chümoukedima (c) Longleng (d) Peren
> **Ans: (b) Chümoukedima.** Niuland and Tseminyü were created at the same time, and Shamator and Meluri later. ⚠️ Current total is **17**; confirm.

**39.** The Doyang Hydro-Electric Project is in which district?  
(a) Kohima (b) Wokha (c) Mon (d) Phek
> **Ans: (b) Wokha.**

**40.** The Dzükou lily (*Lilium chitrangadae*) is endemic to:  
(a) Loktak (b) Dzükou Valley (c) Shilloi Lake (d) Japfü peak
> **Ans: (b) Dzükou Valley**, on the Nagaland–Manipur border.

**41.** Shilloi Lake, shaped like a human footprint, is in:  
(a) Phek (b) Mon (c) Kohima (d) Zunheboto
> **Ans: (a) Phek district.**

**42.** The Guinness-record "tallest rhododendron tree" was found on:  
(a) Mount Saramati (b) Japfü Peak (c) Dzükou (d) Pauna
> **Ans: (b) Japfü Peak**, near Kohima.

**43.** ⚠️ Which Nagaland product was among the first in the North-East to get a GI tag (2008)?  
(a) Naga Tree Tomato (b) Naga Mircha (King Chilli) (c) Chakhesang Shawl (d) Naga Cucumber
> **Ans: (b) Naga Mircha.** (Tree Tomato and Chakhesang Shawl came later.)

**44.** Hornbill Festival started in 2000. The December 2025 edition was therefore the:  
(a) 24th (b) 25th (c) 26th (d) 27th
> **Ans: (c) 26th.** (2000 = 1st, so 2025 = 26th, and **Dec 2026 = 27th**.) ⚠️ Check the 2025 partner country/state.

**45.** Kohima's ancient name, from which "Kohima" is derived, is:  
(a) Kewhira (b) Kisama (c) Jotsoma (d) Khonoma
> **Ans: (a) Kewhira.**

**46.** The Armed Forces (Special Powers) Act that applies in parts of Nagaland was enacted in:  
(a) 1947 (b) 1958 (c) 1963 (d) 1990
> **Ans: (b) 1958.** ⚠️ The areas covered are revised periodically.

**47.** The Konyaks are best known for their traditional:  
(a) terrace rice farming (b) face tattoos and headhunting past, under hereditary chiefs (Anghs) (c) matrilineal inheritance (d) boat-building
> **Ans: (b).**

**48.** Triple Falls, a tourist spot, is at Seithekima in:  
(a) Dimapur/Chümoukedima (b) Mokokchung (c) Wokha (d) Phek
> **Ans: (a)** Chümoukedima (formerly part of Dimapur district).

## PART C — Mental ability (expected item types)

**49.** Find the next number: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?  
(a) 40 (b) 42 (c) 44 (d) 36
> **Ans: (b) 42.** The pattern is n(n+1): 1·2, 2·3, …, 6·7 = 42.

**50.** A, C, F, J, O, ?  
(a) T (b) U (c) V (d) S
> **Ans: (b) U.** The gaps grow +2, +3, +4, +5, +6 (O = 15 → 21 = U).

**51.** The angle between the hands of a clock at 3:40 is:  
(a) 120° (b) 130° (c) 140° (d) 150°
> **Ans: (b) 130°.** Using |30H − 5.5M|: |90 − 220| = 130°.

**52.** 1 January 2025 was a Wednesday. What day was 1 January 2026?  
(a) Wednesday (b) Thursday (c) Friday (d) Tuesday
> **Ans: (b) Thursday.** 2025 is not a leap year, so the day moves on by one.

**53.** If MOTHER is coded as OQVJGT, how is FATHER coded?  
(a) HCVJGT (b) HBVJGT (c) GCVJGT (d) HCUJGT
> **Ans: (a) HCVJGT.** Each letter moves forward by 2.

**54.** Pointing to a man, Asen said, "He is the son of my grandfather's only son." The man is Asen's:  
(a) cousin (b) uncle (c) brother (d) father
> **Ans: (c) brother.** Grandfather's only son = Asen's father, and father's son = brother.

**55.** A man walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, turns right again and walks 5 km. Where is he from the start?  
(a) 3 km west (b) 3 km east (c) 5 km south (d) at the start
> **Ans: (b) 3 km east.**

**56.** A father is three times as old as his son. After 15 years he will be twice as old. The son's present age is:  
(a) 10 (b) 12 (c) 15 (d) 20
> **Ans: (c) 15.** Solve 3S + 15 = 2(S + 15), so S = 15 (and the father is 45).

**57.** If A:B = 2:3 and B:C = 4:5, then A:B:C is:  
(a) 2:3:5 (b) 8:12:15 (c) 4:6:5 (d) 8:12:10
> **Ans: (b) 8:12:15.**

**58.** A train 200 m long runs at 72 km/h. How long does it take to pass a pole?  
(a) 8 s (b) 10 s (c) 12 s (d) 20 s
> **Ans: (b) 10 s.** 72 km/h = 20 m/s, and 200 ÷ 20 = 10 s.

**59.** A can do a job in 10 days and B in 15 days. Working together they finish it in:  
(a) 5 days (b) 6 days (c) 8 days (d) 12.5 days
> **Ans: (b) 6 days.** 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6.

**60.** Odd one out: 121, 144, 169, 190, 225  
(a) 144 (b) 169 (c) 190 (d) 225
> **Ans: (c) 190.** All the others are perfect squares.

## PART D — General English (expected item types)

**61.** Choose the correct option: "Neither the teacher nor the students ___ present."  
(a) was (b) were (c) is (d) has been
> **Ans: (b) were.** With *neither…nor*, the verb agrees with the **nearer** subject (students).

**62.** "Each of the boys ___ given a prize."  
(a) were (b) are (c) was (d) have been
> **Ans: (c) was.** *Each* is singular.

**63.** Change to passive: "The Chief Minister inaugurated the festival."  
(a) The festival is inaugurated by the Chief Minister. (b) The festival was inaugurated by the Chief Minister. (c) The festival has been inaugurated by the Chief Minister. (d) The festival had inaugurated the Chief Minister.
> **Ans: (b).**

**64.** Change to indirect speech: He said, "I am going to Kohima tomorrow."  
(a) He said that he is going to Kohima tomorrow. (b) He said that he was going to Kohima the next day. (c) He said that I was going to Kohima tomorrow. (d) He told that he goes to Kohima the next day.
> **Ans: (b).** *am → was*, *tomorrow → the next day*.

**65.** Fill in the preposition: "She prefers tea ___ coffee."  
(a) than (b) over (c) to (d) from
> **Ans: (c) to.** *Prefer X to Y.*

**66.** Fill in the preposition: "We congratulated him ___ his success."  
(a) for (b) on (c) at (d) about
> **Ans: (b) on.**

**67.** Sentence improvement: "If I **was** a bird, I would fly."  
(a) If I am a bird (b) If I were a bird (c) If I had been a bird (d) No improvement
> **Ans: (b) were.** Use the subjunctive for an unreal condition.

**68.** Fill in the tense: "By next December, she ___ her degree."  
(a) will complete (b) will have completed (c) completes (d) has completed
> **Ans: (b) will have completed** (future perfect).

**69.** Idiom — "**a blessing in disguise**":  
(a) a hidden curse (b) something that seems bad but turns out good (c) a religious gift (d) a false promise
> **Ans: (b).**

**70.** Idiom — "**to hit the nail on the head**":  
(a) to hurt oneself (b) to say or do exactly the right thing (c) to work as a carpenter (d) to fail
> **Ans: (b).**

**71.** One word: *One who knows everything*:  
(a) Omnipotent (b) Omnipresent (c) Omniscient (d) Omnivorous
> **Ans: (c) Omniscient.** (Omnipotent = all-powerful; omnipresent = everywhere.)

**72.** One word: *Handwriting that cannot be read*:  
(a) Illegible (b) Ineligible (c) Illiterate (d) Invisible
> **Ans: (a) Illegible.**

**73.** Synonym of **DILIGENT**:  
(a) Lazy (b) Hard-working (c) Clever (d) Careless
> **Ans: (b) Hard-working.**

**74.** Antonym of **TRANSPARENT**:  
(a) Clear (b) Opaque (c) Bright (d) Visible
> **Ans: (b) Opaque.**

**75.** Choose the correctly spelt word:  
(a) Embarass (b) Embarrass (c) Embaras (d) Embbarass
> **Ans: (b) Embarrass** (double r, double s).
